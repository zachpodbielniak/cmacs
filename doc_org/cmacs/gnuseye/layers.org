#+TITLE: GNU's Eye — Layer Catalogue
#+AUTHOR: Zach Podbielniak
#+OPTIONS: toc:2 num:t

Every shipped data layer, by category.  All layers are *off by default*;
enable them from the toggle pane (left).  Layer files are listed in
~cmacs-gnuseye-layer-files~ and loaded when the globe opens.

* Astronomical

** satellites — ~cmacs-gnuseye-astro.el~
- Source: CelesTrak GP element sets (TLE), no key.
- Endpoint: ~https://celestrak.org/NORAD/elements/gp.php?GROUP=<group>&FORMAT=tle~.
- Refresh: TLEs at most every ~cmacs-gnuseye-astro-tle-ttl~ (6 h, cached);
  positions re-propagated locally every smooth tick (no network).
- Geometry: heading-oriented satellite icon high above the surface + a
  one-orbit ground-track trail.
- Motion: ~:advance~ re-propagates each satellite (two-body Keplerian) to now.
- Config: ~cmacs-gnuseye-astro-group~ (default "stations"),
  ~-astro-max~ (40), ~-astro-show-orbits~ (t), ~-astro-tle-ttl~.

* Air

** aircraft — ~cmacs-gnuseye-air.el~
- Source: adsb.lol, a free keyless community ADS-B aggregator (fallback:
  OpenSky with ~OPENSKY_USER~/~OPENSKY_PASS~).
- Endpoint: ~https://api.adsb.lol/v2/lat/LAT/lon/LON/dist/NM~.
- Refresh: 20 s.  Geometry: little-plane icon at exaggerated altitude with a
  drop-line; ~:cluster~ on (count badges when zoomed out).
- Motion: great-circle dead reckoning between fetches.
- Global sweep: ~cmacs-gnuseye-air-global~ (t) sweeps the whole globe with
  ~-air-global-tiles~ overlapping queries (~-air-global-radius-nm~ 5000)
  merged by ICAO hex, fired in parallel on C worker threads with a fast
  view-local query first.  ~-air-marker-scale~ (0.15), ~-air-source~.

* Marine

** vessels — ~cmacs-gnuseye-marine.el~
- Source: an AIS vessel JSON feed at ~cmacs-gnuseye-marine-url~ (nil = off).
  Free *global* AIS is mostly websocket + key; point this at a regional
  open-AIS REST endpoint or a LAN AIS-catcher / rtl-ais server.
- Refresh: 30 s.  Geometry: hull + superstructure, bow = heading;
  ~:cluster~ on.  Motion: dead reckoning (SOG converted knots → m/s).
- Config: ~cmacs-gnuseye-marine-url~, ~-marine-max~ (300).

* Weather & events

** quakes — ~cmacs-gnuseye-weather.el~
- Source: USGS earthquakes GeoJSON, no key.  Endpoint
  ~.../summary/all_hour.geojson~ (~cmacs-gnuseye-quakes-url~).  Refresh 300 s.
- Geometry: magnitude-sized sphere.

** launches — ~cmacs-gnuseye-weather.el~
- Source: Launch Library 2 upcoming launches, no key (rate-limited).
  ~cmacs-gnuseye-launches-url~.  Refresh 1800 s.  Geometry: rocket on the pad.

** fires — ~cmacs-gnuseye-weather.el~
- Source: NASA FIRMS active fires (CSV); needs ~FIRMS_MAP_KEY~.  Refresh 900 s.
  ~cmacs-gnuseye-fires-source~ (default VIIRS_SNPP_NRT).  Geometry: flame
  sized by fire radiative power.

* Natural events

** natural-events — ~cmacs-gnuseye-natural.el~
- Source: NASA EONET v3 (one rich keyless feed): volcanoes, severe storms,
  wildfires, sea/lake ice & icebergs, floods, landslides, …
- Endpoint: ~https://eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov/api/v3/events?status=open&limit=200~
  (~cmacs-gnuseye-eonet-url~).  Refresh 1800 s.
- Geometry: point at each event's most recent fix; the category maps to a
  marker kind (fire / volcano / storm / event).

** nws-alerts — ~cmacs-gnuseye-natural.el~
- Source: US NWS active alerts (GeoJSON), no key.  *Requires a descriptive
  User-Agent* (~cmacs-gnuseye-nws-user-agent~) — NWS returns 403 otherwise.
- Endpoint: ~https://api.weather.gov/alerts/active~ (~cmacs-gnuseye-nws-url~).
  Refresh 300 s; cap ~cmacs-gnuseye-nws-max~ (150).
- Geometry: each polygon-bearing warning becomes a *filled, severity-coloured
  zone* (~:polygon~) plus a centroid ~alert~ marker.  Extreme→red,
  Severe→orange, Moderate→yellow, Minor→blue.

* Space weather

** aurora — ~cmacs-gnuseye-space.el~
- Source: NOAA SWPC OVATION auroral-oval forecast, no key.
- Endpoint: ~https://services.swpc.noaa.gov/json/ovation_aurora_latest.json~
  (~cmacs-gnuseye-aurora-url~).  Refresh 300 s.
- Geometry: a band of probability-coloured dots (brighter green = higher
  probability).  Config: ~-aurora-threshold~ (25 %), ~-aurora-step~ (2),
  ~-aurora-max~ (600).

* Conflict & geopolitics

** gdelt — ~cmacs-gnuseye-conflict.el~
- Source: GDELT GEO 2.0 geolocated news, no key (~1 req/s).
- Endpoint: ~https://api.gdeltproject.org/api/v2/geo/geo?query=...&format=GeoJSON~.
  Refresh 900 s.  Geometry: a point per location, sized by mention volume;
  ~:cluster~ on.
- Config: ~cmacs-gnuseye-gdelt-query~ (default a conflict query),
  ~-gdelt-timespan~ ("1d"), ~-gdelt-max~ (250).
