#+TITLE: GNU's Eye — Layer Catalogue
#+AUTHOR: Zach Podbielniak
#+OPTIONS: toc:2 num:t

Every shipped data layer, by category.  All layers are *off by default*;
enable them from the toggle pane (left).  Layer files are listed in
~cmacs-gnuseye-layer-files~ and loaded when the globe opens.

* Solar system — ~cmacs-gnuseye-celestial.el~ (v3)

Bodies render at their true geocentric direction (sub-point) on
log-compressed shells (world radius 20 + 10·log10(km/1e5), clamped [12,74]);
the inspector shows real distance + light-time.  ~0~ returns to Earth.

** solar-system
Sun + Moon + Mercury…Neptune from local ephemerides
(~cmacs-gnuseye-ephem.el~: Standish planetary elements + low-precision Moon;
no key, no network, validated against Horizons fixtures).  Positions
recomputed every smooth tick — the sky sweeps live with Earth's rotation.

** probes
Deep-space spacecraft from keyless JPL Horizons (geocentric observer
ephemeris): Voyager 1/2, New Horizons, JWST by default
(~cmacs-gnuseye-probes~).  Refresh 6 h.

** asteroids
Asteroids from Horizons: Apophis, Ceres, Bennu, Eros by default
(~cmacs-gnuseye-asteroids~ — any Horizons small-body designation).
Refresh 6 h.

* Astronomical

** satellites — ~cmacs-gnuseye-astro.el~
- Source: CelesTrak GP element sets (TLE), no key.
- Endpoint: ~https://celestrak.org/NORAD/elements/gp.php?GROUP=<group>&FORMAT=tle~.
- Refresh: TLEs at most every ~cmacs-gnuseye-astro-tle-ttl~ (6 h, cached);
  positions re-propagated locally every smooth tick (no network).
- Geometry: heading-oriented satellite icon high above the surface + a
  one-orbit ground-track trail.
- Motion: ~:advance~ re-propagates each satellite (two-body Keplerian) to now.
- Config: ~cmacs-gnuseye-astro-group~ (default "stations"),
  ~-astro-max~ (40), ~-astro-show-orbits~ (t), ~-astro-tle-ttl~.

* Air

** aircraft — ~cmacs-gnuseye-air.el~
- Source: adsb.lol, a free keyless community ADS-B aggregator (fallback:
  OpenSky with ~OPENSKY_USER~/~OPENSKY_PASS~).
- Endpoint: ~https://api.adsb.lol/v2/lat/LAT/lon/LON/dist/NM~.
- Refresh: 20 s.  Geometry: little-plane icon at exaggerated altitude with a
  drop-line; ~:cluster~ on (count badges when zoomed out).
- Motion: great-circle dead reckoning between fetches.
- Global sweep: ~cmacs-gnuseye-air-global~ (t) sweeps the whole globe with
  ~-air-global-tiles~ overlapping queries (~-air-global-radius-nm~ 5000)
  merged by ICAO hex, fired in parallel on C worker threads with a fast
  view-local query first.  ~-air-marker-scale~ (0.15), ~-air-source~.

* Marine

** vessels — ~cmacs-gnuseye-marine.el~
- Source: live AIS from ~cmacs-gnuseye-marine-url~.  The default is the
  *Digitraffic open AIS* (~https://meri.digitraffic.fi/api/ais/v1/locations~;
  keyless GeoJSON, live Baltic / Gulf of Finland coverage — fly to ~59N 21E~
  to see it).  Free *global* AIS is websocket + key (aisstream.io) or
  regional; point the URL at any other feed — the parser reads GeoJSON
  FeatureCollections (position from geometry) and flat arrays
  (mmsi/lat/lon/cog/sog/name), honouring the AIS not-available sentinels
  (heading 511, cog 360).
- Refresh: 30 s.  Geometry: hull + superstructure, bow = heading, small and
  zoom-scaled to a constant on-screen size (~cmacs-gnuseye-marine-marker-scale~,
  0.15); ~:cluster~ on.  Motion: dead reckoning (SOG converted knots → m/s).
- Config: ~cmacs-gnuseye-marine-url~, ~-marine-urls~ (extra feeds, merged by
  MMSI — widen the regional coverage), ~-marine-max~ (1000),
  ~-marine-marker-scale~.

* Weather & events

** quakes — ~cmacs-gnuseye-weather.el~
- Source: USGS earthquakes GeoJSON, no key.  Endpoint
  ~.../summary/all_hour.geojson~ (~cmacs-gnuseye-quakes-url~).  Refresh 300 s.
- Geometry: magnitude-sized sphere.

** launches — ~cmacs-gnuseye-weather.el~
- Source: Launch Library 2 upcoming launches, no key (rate-limited).
  ~cmacs-gnuseye-launches-url~.  Refresh 1800 s.  Geometry: rocket on the pad.

** fires — ~cmacs-gnuseye-weather.el~
- Source: NASA FIRMS active fires (CSV); needs ~FIRMS_MAP_KEY~.  Refresh 900 s.
  ~cmacs-gnuseye-fires-source~ (default VIIRS_SNPP_NRT).  Geometry: flame
  sized by fire radiative power.

* Natural events

** natural-events — ~cmacs-gnuseye-natural.el~
- Source: NASA EONET v3 (one rich keyless feed): volcanoes, severe storms,
  wildfires, sea/lake ice & icebergs, floods, landslides, …
- Endpoint: ~https://eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov/api/v3/events?status=open&limit=200~
  (~cmacs-gnuseye-eonet-url~).  Refresh 1800 s.
- Geometry: point at each event's most recent fix; the category maps to a
  marker kind (fire / volcano / storm / event).

** nws-alerts — ~cmacs-gnuseye-natural.el~
- Source: US NWS active alerts (GeoJSON), no key.  *Requires a descriptive
  User-Agent* (~cmacs-gnuseye-nws-user-agent~) — NWS returns 403 otherwise.
- Endpoint: ~https://api.weather.gov/alerts/active~ (~cmacs-gnuseye-nws-url~).
  Refresh 300 s; cap ~cmacs-gnuseye-nws-max~ (150).
- Geometry: each polygon-bearing warning becomes a *filled, severity-coloured
  zone* (~:polygon~) plus a centroid ~alert~ marker.  Extreme→red,
  Severe→orange, Moderate→yellow, Minor→blue.

* Space weather

** aurora — ~cmacs-gnuseye-space.el~
- Source: NOAA SWPC OVATION auroral-oval forecast, no key.
- Endpoint: ~https://services.swpc.noaa.gov/json/ovation_aurora_latest.json~
  (~cmacs-gnuseye-aurora-url~).  Refresh 300 s.
- Geometry: a band of probability-coloured dots (brighter green = higher
  probability).  Config: ~-aurora-threshold~ (25 %), ~-aurora-step~ (2),
  ~-aurora-max~ (600).

* Conflict & geopolitics

** gdelt — ~cmacs-gnuseye-conflict.el~
- Source: GDELT GEO 2.0 geolocated news, no key (~1 req/s).
- Endpoint: ~https://api.gdeltproject.org/api/v2/geo/geo?query=...&format=GeoJSON~.
  Refresh 900 s.  Geometry: a point per location, sized by mention volume;
  ~:cluster~ on.
- Config: ~cmacs-gnuseye-gdelt-query~ (default a conflict query),
  ~-gdelt-timespan~ ("1d"), ~-gdelt-max~ (250).

** ucdp (v2) — ~cmacs-gnuseye-conflict.el~
UCDP georeferenced conflict events (GED API, ~cmacs-gnuseye-ucdp-url~;
keyless), sized by deaths; clustered.  Refresh daily.

* Infrastructure — ~cmacs-gnuseye-infra.el~ / ~-keyed.el~

** cables
TeleGeography submarine-cable GeoJSON (~cmacs-gnuseye-cables-url~; no key,
fetched once).  Each cable segment is drawn as a polyline trail.

** ports
A built-in static set of major seaports and maritime chokepoints
(~cmacs-gnuseye-ports~).  Offline, no key.

** bases (in ~-keyed.el~)
A built-in static set of notable military bases and spaceports
(~cmacs-gnuseye-bases~), kinds ~base~ / ~spaceport~.  Offline, no key.

** cyber (v2)
Feodo Tracker C2 IP blocklist (~cmacs-gnuseye-cyber-url~; keyless), geolocated
via the keyless IP resolver in ~cmacs-gnuseye-geoloc~ (capped + cached);
clustered.  Refresh 1 h.

* Health & society — ~cmacs-gnuseye-health.el~

** radiation
Safecast crowdsourced radiation measurements (~cmacs-gnuseye-radiation-url~;
no key).  Sized by reading.

** airquality
OpenAQ latest measurements (~cmacs-gnuseye-airquality-url~).  OpenAQ v3 needs
~OPENAQ_API_KEY~ (declared ~:needs-key~).

** disasters (v2)
ReliefWeb ongoing disasters (~cmacs-gnuseye-disasters-url~; keyless), placed at
the affected country's location.  Refresh 1 h.

* Media & cameras — ~cmacs-gnuseye-media.el~

** iss
The ISS as a moving ~camera~ marker (keyless wheretheiss.at) carrying a
configurable live-stream URL (~cmacs-gnuseye-iss-stream-url~) opened from the
inspector's ~[w]~ action.

** webcams
Live webcams from ~cmacs-gnuseye-webcams-url~ (keyless) or the Windy API
(~WINDY_WEBCAMS_KEY~); ~camera~ markers with a stream URL in ~:data~.

* Conflict & intelligence (keyed / derived)

** acled (in ~-keyed.el~)
ACLED armed-conflict events; needs ~ACLED_KEY~ + ~ACLED_EMAIL~.  Clustered,
sized by fatalities.

** sirens (in ~-keyed.el~)
Regional alert/siren feed from a configurable URL (~cmacs-gnuseye-sirens-url~;
OREF mirror or a civil-alert endpoint).

** hotspots (in ~cmacs-gnuseye-intel.el~)
A *derived* layer: cross-source correlation surfaces grid cells holding two
or more distinct co-located signal kinds, scored by density.  See
[[file:reference.org][reference.org]] (intelligence).

* Non-geospatial panels — ~cmacs-gnuseye-markets.el~

Table feeds rendered as side buffers via ~cmacs-gnuseye-define-panel~ (never
the globe): ~crypto~ (CoinGecko), ~fear-greed~ (alternative.me), ~fx~
(Frankfurter/ECB), ~predictions~ (Polymarket) — all keyless.
~cmacs-gnuseye-dashboard~ opens them all.
